## Visual generation strategy
1. Lock reference sheet: front, side, 3/4 views and expression sheet for each character. Approval required before generating expensive video.
2. Reuse one master environment plate for valley and rainbow; create rainbow-visible and rainbow-gone variants.
3. Animate key actions only: 03 hop, 07 group play, 08 rainbow fade, 10 volunteering, 11 departure.
4. For quieter narration sections use camera parallax, cutaways, and subtle idle animation rather than a single static loop.
5. Preserve 180-degree screen direction and character size across edits.
6. Create separate audio stems: narration, character dialogue, music, effects. Use original/licensed music only.
7. Produce JA and EN versions with matched scene timings; never simply speed up children's dialogue.

## Draft AI image prompt template
"High-quality stylized 3D animated family film, warm soft global illumination, tactile plush fur, colorful but not neon, soft rounded forms, gentle facial expressions, consistent character reference sheet, 16:9 composition, no text, no logos, no watermark. SCENE: {description}. Camera: {shot}. Keep exact reference design."
Negative: "distorted anatomy, extra limbs, inconsistent costumes, scary expressions, harsh flashing, written text, brand logos."

## Acceptance checks
- Recognizable same rabbit and three friends in every shot
- Distinct personalities clear without narration
- Rainbow disappearance gentle, not frightening
- 2-3 minutes without obvious repetitive five-second loops
- Japanese/English speech intelligible; audio levels checked
- Rights checked for video, voice, music, image assets
- Final review by owner before any upload or spend

At 50 credits/5 seconds, 160 seconds fully AI-generated would be 1600 credits before retries. This is arithmetic only; actual model pricing may vary. Budget a 20-30 second pilot of key action first; do not purchase credits or publish without approval.
